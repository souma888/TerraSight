FIELD SAFETY FIRST

Keep your distance. Let the camera do the work.

Capture only from safe, stable ground. Never approach an active landslide, an unstable slope or an exposed edge to improve image coverage. Terra Sight is an experimental analysis tool, not an early-warning system or a replacement for a qualified geotechnical assessment.
