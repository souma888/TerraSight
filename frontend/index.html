    FIELD NOTES · CAPTURE GUIDE

    Good coverage starts safely.

    Small, consistent camera moves make a better image sequence.

    FIELD GUIDE · 01 / 04

    Walk the view.
    Not the slope.

    Stay on stable ground and move laterally through a short arc. Keep recognizable terrain features in consecutive frames so the pipeline can try to match them.

    01⌖

    Choose a safe viewpoint

    Start far enough away to include the full area. Never move onto unstable ground or closer to an active landslide for a better view.

    02↔

    Move in short steps

    Shift sideways a little between each photo. Aim for about 70% overlap and keep the camera pointed at the same terrain.

    03◉

    Keep images sharp

    Pause before each shot. Avoid fast panning, digital zoom, blur, deep shadows and large exposure changes.

    04◇

    Cover hidden surfaces

    Use more than one safe angle where possible. Vegetation, ridges and shadows can hide parts of the terrain from a single pass.

    Safety comes before completeness.
